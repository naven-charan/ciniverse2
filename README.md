# 🎬 Ceniverse — Experience Cinema Before You Book

**Ceniverse** is a next-generation movie ticket booking platform designed to transform the way people experience cinema. From choosing theatres and selecting seats to previewing the actual seat view and ordering snacks, Ceniverse brings the entire cinema booking experience into one platform.

Why just book a movie ticket when you can experience your seat before entering the theatre?

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎟️ Seamless Movie Ticket Booking
- Browse available movies and showtimes.
- Explore and select your preferred theatres.
- Book movie tickets with ease.

### 💺 Interactive Seat Selection
- View the theatre seating layout.
- Select your preferred seats.
- Check seat availability while booking.

### 👀 Seat POV Preview
- Experience the Point of View (POV) from your selected seat.
- Preview what the cinema screen looks like from your chosen position.
- Make informed seating decisions before confirming your booking.

### 🍿 In-App Snack Ordering
- Browse available food and beverages.
- Add snacks to your order while booking tickets.
- Enjoy a convenient cinema food-ordering experience.

### 🏢 Theatre Selection
- Explore different theatres.
- Compare available showtimes and seating options.
- Choose the cinema that suits your preferences.

---

## 🚀 What Makes Ceniverse Special?

Traditional ticket booking platforms allow users to select seats based primarily on a seating layout.

**Ceniverse takes it further.**

With its Seat POV Preview feature, users can visualize their cinema experience before making a booking decision.

Our goal is to make movie booking more interactive, convenient, and immersive.
